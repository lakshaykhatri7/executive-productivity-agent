How this brief is built

Every item below traces back to one or more of: the Monday leadership sync transcript, five email threads, two personal voice memos, and five calendars — nothing here is invented. Where a deadline was mentioned more than once (e.g. the vendor list slipped three times across a meeting, an email, and a voice note), the agent merges those mentions into one commitment and keeps only the latest agreed deadline, rather than showing three separate to-dos.

  • My action vs waiting on is assigned from who made the promise: if Arjun said "I'll send/confirm/get you," it's his action; if someone else promised Arjun something, it's on them.
  • Ownership gaps (the Mumbai lease sign-off) are surfaced as flags, never auto-assigned to a person the data doesn't name.
  • A commitment restated in a later voice memo after it was already resolved by email (the Meridian call time) is shown as stale — superseded rather than reopened.
  • This prototype hard-codes the extracted commitments from the assignment data pack so the logic is transparent and inspectable. In production, an LLM extraction pass would populate this same structured schema from live inboxes/calendars/transcripts on a schedule, with the rest of the reasoning (dedup, ownership, deadline tracking) working exactly as shown here.
